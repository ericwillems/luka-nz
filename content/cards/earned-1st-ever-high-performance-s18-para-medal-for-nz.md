I am the first New Zealand swimmer with Down Syndrome to compete in a high performance NZ team internationally. I am also the first S18 swimmer with DS to win a medal for NZ at the Virtus Asia Oceania Games in Brisbane. I have opened this pathway for other swimmers with Down Syndrome.

I am a great lover of all the Arts including music, dance, drama and theatre arts. I also like architecture around the world.


I am into dancing and have grooved for many years with StarJam. I have made many public performances.


You will see many of my YouTube clips include me dancing.


I am great at using sign Language with music which is inspiring for others


I enjoy going to galleries and looking at street art. You'll see I've even seen an exhibition of Banksy Art.


I think nature can be art too

## New Zealand representative S18 swimmers

## Earned 1st ever High performance S18 para medal for NZ

I am the first New Zealand swimmer with Down Syndrome to compete in a high performance NZ team internationally. I am also the first S18 swimmer with DS to win a medal for NZ at the Virtus Asia Oceania Games in Brisbane. I have opened this pathway for other swimmers with Down Syndrome.

![](images/new-zealand-representative/new-zealand-representative-02.jpg)

## First National and International S18 swimming representative for NZ

I have represented New Zealand as an S18 swimmer in many International events including Virtus and DS Worlds.

I have swum for NZ in Australia, France, Portugal and Turkey.

I also have many swimming achievements nationally. Take a look at my photo.

![](images/new-zealand-representative/new-zealand-representative-03.jpg)

## NZ/Oceania record holder

I hold many NZ swim records. I also hold Oceania records in both Long Course and Short Course

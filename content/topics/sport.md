## I'll try any sport


Take a look at my photo carousel. It shows you I'm keen to try any sport. You'll see me swimming, playing basketball, football and tennis. I'm also keen on adventure sport - I'm often out on a kayak or paddle board, skiing the slopes of Cardrona or flying through the skies on a zipline. I hike, bike and jetty jump just for sporty fun. If it's inclusive I'm even happier.

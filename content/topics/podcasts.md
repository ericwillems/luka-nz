## Podcast

## Life with Luka


My [podcast at PlainsMedia](https://plains.org.nz/programme/LifeWithLuka) airs every 2 weeks on a Monday evening. I tell the story of my life and all the things that I do. I have included podcasts about where I have been, what I have studied, my successes in sport and advocacy and so much more. It’s amazing to hear myself speak. I've even interviewed the Mayor!


I include 4-5 songs in each episode because ... it’s really a radio show.


Joshua’s First Interview at Plains Media 🎙️


Hello everyone!


I’m Luka, and I’m excited to share my first interview with my friend Joshua at Plains Media.


Joshua was a little nervous at the beginning, as this was his first time doing an interview. I asked him a few questions, and he did a fantastic job! It was great to see how happy and excited he was to be part of the interview.


I also uploaded the interview to YouTube as a podcast, and it has already received over 156 views! 🎉


Thank you so much to everyone who has watched, supported, and shared the video. I really appreciate it.


If you haven’t watched it yet, please check it out, give it a like, leave a comment, and share it with your friends and family. Your support helps me reach more people and continue creating more interviews and podcasts.


Thank you for supporting me and my podcast journey! ❤️
